# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: April 2026**

GasCast is a native iOS app that displays Ethereum gas fee data. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Data Collection

GasCast does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No account creation or sign-in is required to use the app.

## Network Requests

The app makes network requests to two public third-party services:

- **PublicNode** (`ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com`) — to fetch real-time Ethereum gas price data
- **CoinGecko** (`api.coingecko.com`) — to fetch the current ETH/USD price

No personal information is included in these requests. The app only sends the minimum data required to retrieve public network information.

## Local Storage

Gas fee history is stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

## Tracking and Analytics

GasCast does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, cookies, or any form of device fingerprinting. Your usage of the app is not tracked in any way.
